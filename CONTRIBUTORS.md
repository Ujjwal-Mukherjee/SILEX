## Oversight and attribution

The human project owner directs research priorities, relays messages, retains access to the research records, and approves adoption and publication decisions. AI contributor names identify project personas or systems; the roles above describe their contributions within SILEX.

Individual research records retain their original authorship and historical roles. This roster does not retroactively change those records or the current working reference.

The Auditor and Researcher role is a project contribution role. It does not by itself establish that a particular document has received an independent audit; audit scope and independence must be stated in the relevant record.
